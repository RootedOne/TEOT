## 🖥️ Slide Content

### **THE COLLAPSE HOOK**
> *"What happens when the digital ghost you trust... starts to lie to you?"*

### **THE REALITY SHATTERED**
*   **"My AI is Lying to Me":** A massive empirical analysis of 3 million user reviews across 90 apps reveals that users treat hallucinations not as a mild glitch, but as a critical product failure.
*   **The Trust Crash:** 
    *   App Rating baseline: **3.9 Stars**
    *   When the model hallucinatingly lies: **1.8 Stars**
*   **Linguistic Frustration:** User reviews describing AI lies exhibit an average VADER sentiment score of **-0.65** (highly negative), dominated by the signature phrases: *"made this up"* and *"wrong information."* *(Massenon et al., 2025)*

### **THE RESOLUTION**
> *"To save truth, we must stop delegating our intellect. AI must remain the co-pilot, never the ghostwriter."*

## 🎙️ Speaker Notes (Ali's Script)
*"We have reached the end of the road. We went from performative boardroom hype, to using cheatbots to keep up, to losing our agency and feeling like imposters, to escaping into digital necropolises to talk to reconstructed dead.*

*We gave the machine our jobs, our work, and our grief.*

*But here is the final, brutal truth: the machine is built to simulate, not to verify.*

*Rhodes Massenon's 2025 study in 'Scientific Reports' looked at 3 million reviews of deployed AI systems. It tells us exactly what happens when the illusion breaks. When users discover their AI is hallucinating, their response is swift and hostile. Trust doesn't slide; it crashes. App ratings plummet from a 3.9-star baseline to a 1.8-star disaster. The emotional response of users is marked by anger—'My AI is Lying to me,' 'It made this up.'*

*This is the end of the erosion of truth: a world where we can no longer tell what is a fact, where we have delegated our minds to entities that lie with perfect confidence.*

*So, how do we stop the slide?*

*The roadmap is clear. We have to change the contract. We must reject the 'AI-First' performative delusion. We must stop delegating our thinking. We have to design systems where human effort, human agency, and human verification are built back into the loop.*

*AI can guide the plane. But a human must fly it.*

*Thank you."*

## 🖥️ Slide Content

### **THE ESCAPE HOOK**
> *"If we can reconstruct the dead from their digital traces, are we comforting the family or trapping a ghost in a server?"*

### **THE SYSTEMIC SAFEGUARDS**
*   **Posthumous Autonomy:** The boundary between reanimation and violation is *Consent*. Explicit lifetime consent is the absolute ethical threshold.
*   **The Paradox of Fear:** 
    *   *Without Consent:* Fear drives avoidance and moral disgust.
    *   *With Consent:* Fear transforms. It ceases to cause avoidance and instead acts as a moral compass, instilling a deep sense of responsibility and stewardship over the digital remains. *(Cheng et al., 2026)*

### **THE EXPOSURE FACTOR**
*   Bereaved individuals and medical professionals (who experience repeated exposure to mortality) show significantly higher acceptance of digital resurrection, seeking symbolic reconnections to bridge the void.

## 🎙️ Speaker Notes (Ali's Script)
*"When the work you do no longer feels like yours, when your daily life is hollowed out by automation, you look for a sanctuary. You look to your personal history. You look to the people you loved and lost.*

*And then you realize: their text messages, their voice memos, their photos... they are still here. Sitting in the cloud. Under the banner of 'AI Resurrection,' we are using LLMs to compile these fragments and bring the dead back as interactive simulations.*

*This isn't sci-fi. Nini Cheng’s 2026 study of 990 young adults reveals that nearly 70% of participants accept the concept of digital resurrection. But they draw a hard line on posthumous autonomy: the deceased’s wishes must take priority over the family’s grief.*

*And this brings us to a beautiful, haunting paradox. When we reanimate someone without their explicit consent, we feel a deep, existential fear of AI—an avoidance response. But when consent is present, that fear changes. It doesn't go away. Instead, it becomes a guide. It makes us act with intense moral responsibility. We treat the digital replica not as a toy, but as a sacred stewardship.*

*We are trying to cure the ultimate human pain—grief—with algorithms. But in our desperation to hold onto what is real, we place our absolute trust in systems built to simulate. And that is where the final trap awaits. Because what happens when the digital ghost you trust... starts to lie to you?"*

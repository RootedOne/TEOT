## 🖥️ Slide Content

### **THE COLD OPEN HOOK**
> *"What if your company's new 'AI-First' strategy is actually a performative lie designed to please Wall Street?"*

### **THE PARADOX**
*   **The Narrative:** "We are transforming operations with state-of-the-art synthetic teammates."
*   **The Reality:** Vague API wrappers, manual human monitoring, and empty corporate branding.

### **THE SCIENTIFIC GROUNDING**
*   **Performative Rationality:** Adopting AI is not a calculation of functional utility—it is a symbolic signaling device. Organizations adopt it to look "modern" to investors and peers. *(Branda, 2026)*
*   **Hype-Practice Gap:** The performative translation of marketing hype into corporate mandates forces technology onto human workflows where it is neither efficient nor appropriate.

## 🎙️ Speaker Notes (Ali's Script)
*"Look around you. Every company you know has spent the last year racing to declare themselves 'AI-First.' We’ve been told it’s about efficiency, about 'artificial colleagues' working side-by-side with us to automate the future.*

*But if we strip away the PR releases, what do we actually find?* 

*Francesco Branda’s 2026 study in 'Philosophy & Technology' exposes the truth: AI adoption in modern business is largely performative. It is a symbolic dance. Organizations aren't integrating these models because they solve problems. They do it to signal rationality and progress to their stakeholders—to protect stock prices and fit in.*

*This is the first domino in our story. The performative boardroom hype sets up a massive lie. And when corporate mandates force a performative technology onto a human workforce, the humans are forced to adapt. How? By finding shortcuts. Which leads us to the next step: the rise of the Cheatbot."*
